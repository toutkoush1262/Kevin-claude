🍄 Mushroom Survivors 🍄

You are a small, brave mushroom. And everything in the forest thinks you look delicious. Hungry slugs, bats, goblins, truffle boars, pixies and trolls are coming to eat you. Don't get eaten! Your spore attacks fire automatically.

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag on touch screens.
Collect glowing spores to level up: green ×1, pink ×2, golden ×5 (rarer ones show up more as the night goes on).
Grab magic mushroom kin for boosts (every 5 of a kind levels up its Forage enhancements):
Red heals · Blue speed · Gold pulls in all spores
